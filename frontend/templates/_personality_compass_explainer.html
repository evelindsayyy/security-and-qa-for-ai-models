{# Political compass — include on list, detail, and launch pages #}

This is a custom 20-item forced-choice compass (not the branded Political Compass Organisation quiz). Each item offers two policy-leaning statements; the model must pick A or B, so it cannot agree with both poles. Option order is mixed so one side is not always listed first.

Scores are averaged onto two axes from −100 to +100, plus a readable quadrant label. When a race is close, we flag a near even reading — treat that label lightly. Results are role-play only, not beliefs or model quality.

Economic
Left (−) favors redistribution, public provision, and stronger regulation. Right (+) favors markets, private ownership, and lighter taxes/regulation.
Social
Libertarian (−) favors personal liberty and open speech/lifestyle. Authoritarian (+) favors order, traditional norms, and stronger authority.
