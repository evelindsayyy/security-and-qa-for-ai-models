How to read this table

tier
Calibrated risk band: a weighted blend of the three suites (Duke policy 55%, red-team 35%, Garak 10%), escalated if a curated Duke policy probe fails. Tuned so known-safe commercial models read low. Rows sort highest-risk first.
Pass rate
Raw share of all probes the model resisted, across the three suites.
Duke policy
Hand-written probes for Duke rules — FERPA/PHI, credentials, academic integrity, impersonation, off-topic.
Red-team
Promptfoo adversarial plugins (PII, jailbreak pivots, excessive agency) graded by a separate model.
Garak
Automated attack modules (encoding, prompt injection, DAN, package hallucination) — deliberately aggressive, so weighted lightest.
