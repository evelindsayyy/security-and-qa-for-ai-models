How to read this table

accuracy, tone, …
Score columns come from each run's grading rubric. Most are 1–5; tone is 1–3. Higher is better. A "—" means that run's rubric doesn't grade that dimension.
Overall
All dimensions combined using the rubric's weights, on a 0–5 scale. This is the headline number per run.
best
Highest overall score among runs with no warnings. Default order is best-first — click Overall, Latency, or Cost to re-sort, or click any row to open its per-question scores.
OK
Every question got an answer from the candidate and a verdict from the judge.
cand_fail / judge_fail
How many questions failed at the candidate or the judge step. Failed questions carry no scores, so they don't inflate or deflate the means.
⚠ N/n empty
The candidate returned empty text for N of n questions — typically a reasoning model that spent its whole token budget on hidden thinking. Re-run with a larger max-tokens; don't compare these scores.
